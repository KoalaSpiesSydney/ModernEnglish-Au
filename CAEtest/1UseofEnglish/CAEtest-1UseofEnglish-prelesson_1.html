Key phrases and structures

Common expressions

1

As + adjective / adverb + as

Meaning

Comparing two things equally.

Examples

  • The film wasn’t as interesting as I expected.
  • She didn’t speak as confidently as her classmates.

Variations

not nearly as … as almost as … as twice as … as
2

Yet to be + past participle

Meaning

Something has not happened so far.

Examples

  • The results are yet to be announced.
  • The problem is yet to be solved.

Variations

still to be … has / have not been … yet
3

Pick up (a language or skill)

Meaning

Learn something informally or gradually.

Examples

  • She picked up Spanish while travelling.
  • You can pick up coding by watching tutorials.

Variations

pick up on (notice) pick up the pace (go faster)
4

At the same time

Meaning

Simultaneously; also used to introduce a contrasting idea.

Examples

  • You can study and work at the same time.
  • The app is useful; at the same time, it can be distracting.
5

Little doubt

Meaning

Almost certain.

Examples

  • There is little doubt the show will be renewed.
  • Experts have no doubt the trend will continue.
6

Insist on + -ing

Meaning

Demand something firmly.

Examples

  • She insisted on meeting in person.
  • They insist on checking every detail.

Variation

insist that + clause
7

Recommend + -ing

Meaning

Suggest something.

Examples

  • I recommend trying the new café.
  • She recommended reading the article.

Variation

recommend that + subject + base verb
8

Advise + -ing

Meaning

Suggest or warn.

Examples

  • The coach advised practising daily.
  • Doctors advise getting enough sleep.
9

Complain about + -ing

Meaning

Express dissatisfaction.

Examples

  • Customers complained about waiting too long.
  • She complained about being ignored.
10

Keep (on) + -ing

Meaning

Continue doing something.

Examples

  • They kept on playing despite the rain.
  • He keeps forgetting his keys.
