1 · The present perfect for experience

The song asks Have you ever seen Sydney from a 747? We use the present perfect to talk about experiences in our life up to now, when the exact time isn’t important.
have / has + past participle
I’ve flown to Sydney.  ·  She hasn’t been to Bondi.  ·  Have you ever swum in the ocean?

Write the verbs in brackets in the present perfect.

I (see) Sydney from the air, and it looks amazing at night.

you ever (fly) on a 747?

She (be) to Bondi Beach three times.

We (not / swim) at Bondi yet — the water is too cold!

He (fall) in love with someone who lives far away.

I (write) her so many letters.

They (spend) all their savings on plane tickets.

she ever (leave) her job for love?

2 · Past participles

Regular verbs add -ed: land → landed, travel → travelled.
Irregular verbs change — you have to learn them: see → saw → seen.

3 · Present perfect or past simple?

Present perfect – life experience or unfinished time: ever, never, yet, already, before, since, for.
Past simple – a finished time in the past: yesterday, last year, in 2019, two days ago, when…
I’ve been to Sydney.  but  I went to Sydney last summer.

Choose the correct form.

I the Opera House at night.

Our plane at 6 o’clock this morning.

on a jumbo jet?

He his job two weeks ago.

She in Sydney since 2020.

When I a child, I lived near the beach.

I to Bondi twice, but I can’t surf!

We to each other every week in 2019.

4 · Your turn · Have you ever…?

Ask a partner. If they say yes, ask a follow-up question in the past simple: When did you go? What was it like?

  • Have you ever seen a city from a plane at night?
  • Have you ever been to Bondi Beach — or another famous beach?
  • Have you ever done something crazy for love?
  • Have you ever quit a job or left home for a big change?
  • Have you ever written a letter by hand?

5 · Write a verse about your city

The song shows Sydney from the window of a plane. Now write a short verse about your own city, or a city you love. Choose your city and where you are looking from — your first line builds itself underneath.

Your verse

Make it better: start with Have you ever seen…? (present perfect) · use places — on my left, on my right, below me, in the distance · add colours and sounds · finish with your feelings.

Have you ever seen Melbourne from a ferry on the bay?
On my left, the city towers shine silver in the rain.
On my right, the beach huts stand like crayons in a row.
It’s early, and the sky is pink and gold.
I feel calm, because this city has always felt like home.

Read your verse to a partner. Can they guess the city before you say its name?
