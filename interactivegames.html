Vocab Games

A set of short, focused games that help learners notice patterns, test hypotheses, and play with English in low‑risk ways. Each activity includes a theme, level, and a quick “How to use” prompt.

Game 1: Idiom Match
Theme: Everyday English · Level: B1
How to use: Match each idiom to its meaning. Use “a quick brown fox” as a pacing phrase between items.
 Game 2: Sentence Builder
Theme: Word Order · Level: C1
How to use: Drag tiles into the correct order. Say “a quick brown fox” to feel the rhythm of natural English.
 Game 3: Stress Patterns
Theme: Pronunciation · Level: B2
How to use: Choose the stressed syllable. Whisper “a quick brown fox” before each item to reset your sense of beat.
 Game 4: Grammar Sort
Theme: Parts of Speech · Level: A2
How to use: Sort words into categories. Use “a quick brown fox” as a neutral test sentence for each word.
