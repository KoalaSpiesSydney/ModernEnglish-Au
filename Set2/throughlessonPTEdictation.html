Before You Start

PTE tip: in Write from Dictation you score one point for every word spelled correctly, so one wrong -ough- word costs a point.

These words look almost the same but sound completely different. Listen to each one, then check the meaning.

WordListenMeaning and example
thoughbut; despite that. She enjoyed it, though it was long.
throughin one side and out the other; by means of. Walk through the door.
throughoutin every part of; for the whole time. Throughout the lecture.
thorough · thoroughlycomplete and careful. A thorough analysis.
thoughtpast of think; an idea. Food for thought.

How to practise: play each sentence once, as in the test, and type it. Then listen again and fix your answer before you press Submit. Five focused minutes a day is better than one long session.

Write from Dictation

Press play and type the whole sentence. Punctuation, apostrophes and capital letters are ignored; every word must be spelled correctly.
