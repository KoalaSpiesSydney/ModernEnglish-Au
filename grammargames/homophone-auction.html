How to Play

Beginner · Intermediate · Advanced

Every sentence is for sale. Some are forgeries.

Teams become wealthy families and bid on sentences they believe are genuine. A forgery hides a homophone error, such as it's for its or their for there.

One person is the auctioneer: the teacher or a student. The auctioneer runs this screen, reads each lot aloud, taps a family's paddle when they bid, and calls "Going once, going twice, sold!"

Read the catalogue first and agree as a team before you raise your paddle. Nobody finds out what they bought until the last lot is sold.

At the reveal, the class votes on each lot before the answer appears. A family that bought a forgery scores double points if it can correct the error in the redemption round.

Genuine lot = 1 point · corrected forgery = 2 points. The family with the most points tops the Rich List.
