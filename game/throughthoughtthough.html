How to Play

Level B1–B2

Nine tiles: four matching pairs and one question with no partner.

Flip two tiles to match each sentence with the word that fills the gap — and the job that word is doing.

The black tiles are the definitions; the grey tiles are the gap-fill sentences.

When the four pairs are done, the odd tile turns over on its own: that one is for you and your partner to answer out loud.

New sentences every time you reset.
