How to play

1, 2 or 3 players
  1. It works like solitaire with a pack of cards. All the tiles start face down.
  2. Turn over two tiles. If you have found an idiom and its matching meaning, they stay face up and the pair is yours — have another turn.
  3. If they do not match, they turn back over and it is the next player’s turn.
  4. Play until every pair has been found.

Keep your own score. The counter on the board shows how many pairs have been found altogether, not how many each player has won — so remember your own total. Most pairs at the end wins.

Start at Level 1. Each level uses harder idioms and a bigger grid.
