How to play
  • Tap a word to drop it into the highlighted gap, or drag it into any gap.
  • On a keyboard, press 1, 2 or 3 to choose a word.
  • Stuck? Tap Hint (or press H) to see what each word means. The clock keeps running.
  • Clear a set and the next one appears. One wrong word, or running out of time, ends your turn.
  • Earn a medal every 25 points. The first player to reach the target wins. If nobody gets there, the highest score after all turns wins.
