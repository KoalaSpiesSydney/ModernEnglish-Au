    How to play

    Match each body idiom with its meaning. Choose B2 or C1 first.

    1 player · solitaire

    Turn over two cards. If they match, they stay face up. If not, they turn back. Match all 8 pairs in as few turns as you can – the turn counter keeps track.

    2 or more players · snap

    1. Take turns. On your turn, turn over two cards.
    2. If they match, you win the pair (1 point) and go again.
    3. If they don’t match, it’s the next player’s turn. Remember where the cards were!
    4. When every pair is found, the player with the most points wins.

    Choose a score keeper. The game doesn’t keep each player’s score, so pick one person to write the points on paper or the board.
