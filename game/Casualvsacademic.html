How to play

Casual vs Academic Language

  1. Flip two tiles: a light casual tile and a dark academic tile.
  2. If they say the same thing, it's a pair – they stay face up. If not, they turn back over.
  3. Count your pairs. Find all 8 to win. Playing in teams? Take turns and keep score – the most pairs wins.
  4. Then try the gap-fill below using the academic language.

Why tone matters

The same idea can sound chatty or professional. Choosing the right tone (or register) is marked in exams and noticed at work – a casual phrase in an essay or report can cost you marks or make you sound less expert.

IELTS WritingPTE AcademicAcademic EnglishBusiness English
CasualThe prices went up a lot.
AcademicThe costs increased significantly.
  • Swap vague words (a lot, big, things) for precise ones (considerable, substantial, factors).
  • Avoid contractions – it is, not it's.
  • Choose one formal verb over a phrasal verb – increase, not go up.
