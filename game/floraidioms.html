Teacher Notes / Full Idiom List

An idiom is a group of words with a meaning you can’t guess from the single words: Don’t beat around the bush has nothing to do with bushes – it means say it directly. All the idioms here come from the world of plants: trees, flowers, fruit and vegetables.

    Complete the Sentences

    Read each sentence and type the missing plant idiom (for example spill the beans), then press Submit. You may need to change the verb a little, e.g. barking instead of bark.

    How to play

    Flip two cards to find a matching pair. Look at the pictures to help you!

    🌼 Elementary: similes

    A simile compares two things with as … as. Match the start with its ending — as red as … a beetroot!

    🌳 Intermediate to Advanced: idioms

    An idiom has a meaning you can’t guess from the words. Match each idiom with its meaning — bark up the wrong tree … blame the wrong person!

    Choose your level, then each round picks 6 new pairs from a big list — play again and again!
