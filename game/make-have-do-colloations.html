Have, Make & Do

BUILD THE COLLOCATION
  1. Tap a verb tile — have, make or do. They stay on the board.
  2. Turn over a numbered card to see the word.
  3. If the two go together, you keep the card.
  4. If they don't, the card turns back over — remember where it was!
do + the dishes → ✓ we say “do the dishes”
make + a shower → ✗ we say “have a shower”
Clear all 20 cards to finish the board.
