How to play

  • 🪦The answers stay face up. Tap one, then turn over the headstone that hides its meaning.
  • ★Each pair scores 10 points, and the ★ bonus ones score 20.
  • 🐇One headstone hides a lucky rabbit's foot: find it and every point after that is doubled.
  • But beware: another hides a black cat. Turn it over and your turn is over!
  • ⏳Each team has 1 minute to find all 7 pairs, or the monsters take all your points.

Who dares to play?

Teachers: vocabulary to pre-teach before the game

Each round picks 7 pairs at random, so it helps if students already know the core words. Words marked ★ are the bonus trivia words: pre-teach them for a weaker class, or leave them as a challenge.

A quick pre-teach (10 minutes)

  1. Show the three groups and ask students which words they already know.
  2. Drill the tricky pronunciations together (see the teacher notes).
  3. Read a meaning aloud and let pairs race to say the word.
  4. Then play the game: the meanings under the headstones are the same ones.
