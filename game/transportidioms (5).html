Teacher Notes / Full Idiom List

An idiom is a group of words with a meaning you can’t guess from the single words: We’re all in the same boat has nothing to do with boats – it means we all have the same problem. All the idioms here come from the world of transport: cars, trains, boats, planes and roads.

    Complete the Sentences

    Read each sentence and type the missing transport idiom (for example hit the road), then press Submit. You may need to change the verb a little, e.g. missed instead of miss.

    How to play

    Flip two cards to find a matching pair. Look at the pictures to help you! Match all six pairs to arrive at your destination. 📍

    🚲 Elementary: transport phrases

    Some words always go together. Match the start of each phrase with its ending — fasten … your seatbelt!

    🚂 Intermediate to Advanced: idioms

    An idiom has a meaning you can’t guess from the words. Match each idiom with its meaning — in the same boat … in the same difficult situation!

    Choose your level, then each round picks 6 new pairs from a big list — play again and again!
