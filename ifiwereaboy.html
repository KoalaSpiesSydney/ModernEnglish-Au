1 · The second conditional

We use it for imaginary situations now or in the future — things that are not true or not likely.
If + past simple,  would + base verb
If I had wings, I would fly.  ·  With I / he / she, we often say were: If I were you…

2 · Which conditional?

Zero – always true: If you heat water, it boils.
First – a real possibility: If it rains, I’ll stay home.
Second – imaginary now: If I were taller, I’d play basketball.
Third – imaginary past: If I had known, I would have called.
