about justine

English Teacher · Former IELTS Examiner · Learning Designer · Sydney

Hi, I’m Justine Leigh Kelly. I run Modern English Australia from Sydney, under my small studio Koala Spies Sydney. Every game, test and lesson on this site is designed and checked by a qualified, experienced English teacher. For now that is me, drawing on years in English classrooms and IELTS exam rooms; as the site grows, I’ll be bringing in other qualified, experienced teachers based in English-speaking countries. Everything here is free to use, with no account needed.

Why I built this site

I got tired of outdated classroom materials and the poor quality of so many free resources online: games with mistakes in the English, tests that didn’t match the real exam, and pages buried under pop-ups.

It took more time to find something suitable than to code my own games, especially now that I had some AI tools. (I design on paper, code and create, but AI tools create the prototypes.)

Meanwhile, the ed-tech industry is emerging full of developers trying to replace teachers’ agency with AI tools. It shouldn’t be the teachers being replaced by AI created by developers; it should be the teachers developing. I’m extremely happy to do workshops and teach teachers this.

Teachers, let’s make a movement: we train the AI, the AI doesn’t train us. We will not be replaced; we will be more efficient.

Teachers don’t need to be giving away intellectual property, sharing their work on clouds and paying subscriptions for the privilege.

All my apps can be played in the browser and the teacher tools can be downloaded and saved on the teacher’s device.

This saves time, money and the environment.

And all of this could work for a school too, freeing up administration and teacher time, so expert teachers can do what they do best – create engaging lessons (not sort through websites).

I can create bespoke teacher administration documents, curriculum, lessons or just activities that can be plugged into your existing Moodle.

To stay across what is changing in English testing and educational technology, I have been to every relevant industry seminar I could, from Duolingo to Pearson. I write about what I learn on the blog.

From journalism to learning design

Before teaching, I spent several years living and travelling through Latin America and Asia as a freelance journalist, writing for publications including Virgin’s inflight magazine, Private Air Magazine and Australian Traveller. You can read some of that work in my writing portfolio. That is where I first learned to work in the back end of websites, and it is why clear writing matters so much to me.

I hold a Master of Media Arts & Production from UTS and a UX Design Professional Certificate from Microsoft. Today I combine teaching with learning design, building the tools on this site in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript so they load quickly and work on any phone.

How the materials are made

Lessons and games are written for a clear level, from A1 to C2, and practice tests follow the format and timing of the real exams. The teacher tools run in your browser: the text and files you put into them stay on your device and are never sent to me. Ads help keep the site free, and you can read how they work in the privacy policy.

The practice tests on this site are independent. IELTS, PTE and Cambridge are trademarks of their owners, who have not produced or endorsed these materials. If you spot a mistake, please tell me and I’ll fix it.
