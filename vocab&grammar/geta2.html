Before you start

Why do we say get?

Get is very common in English. In everyday English, people often say get, not become, receive or arrive.

become tired get tired
receive a message get a message
arrive home get home

How to play

This game is like the children's card game Memory.

  1. Turn over two cards: one light card (a get expression) and one dark card (a meaning).
  2. A get expression and its meaning? That's a pair – the cards change colour, you hear a click, and they stay open.
  3. Not a pair? The cards turn back. Remember where they are!
  4. Find all 6 pairs to finish the round.

Who can play?

  • One player – play alone.
  • Two teams or more – take turns. The game does not count for each team, so count your own pairs the old-school way: with pen and paper!
  • Press New Round for new cards. You can play again and again.

After the game

There is a practice exercise under the game. What does get mean in each sentence? Drag become, receive or arrive on top. Be careful with the tense: get is present, got is past. Then listen and write three sentences.

Mini Lesson: Three Ways to Use Get

Get is one of the most common verbs in English. Look at the word after it – that tells you what get means.

1 · get = become

get + adjective

You change – your body, feelings or situation is different now.

  • I get hungry at 11 o'clock. (become hungry)
  • It's late. I'm getting tired.
  • She got lost in the city.

Test: can you say become? → become hungry ✓

2 · get = receive / obtain

get + a thing (noun)

Something comes to you, or you go and find it.

  • I got a message from Tom. (received)
  • Let's get some food. (go and find)
  • He gets paid on Friday.

Test: can you say receive or buy / find? → receive a message ✓

3 · phrasal verbs

get + up / on / off / in / out / to

Your body moves or you arrive somewhere. The small word shows the direction.

  • I get up at 7. (out of bed)
  • Get on the bus. Get off at the park.
  • What time do you get home? (arrive)

Remember: bus, train, plane → on / off · car, taxi → in / out of

In the game, matched cards show which get it is: become, receive or phrasal verb.
