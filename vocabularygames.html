Interactive Learning

vocab games

Themes· Idioms· Collocations· Phrasal Verbs

These vocabulary games were designed to reflect the connections of old-school board games or game shows. They can be projected onto a board or wall, played on a tablet in the middle of a table, or played individually on a phone.

Variety of Ways to Use

Teachers can also stand at the front of the class and play the role of a game-show host, turning the activity into a fun whole-class game.

Use them as icebreakers, warmers, revision activities, or homework to reinforce and extend learning.

There is a QR code in the top-left corner of each game, so teachers can project the games while students play along on their phones.
