Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Modern English Australia (modernenglish.au) is a free English learning site run by Koala Spies Sydney in Sydney, Australia. This page explains what information the site collects, how cookies and advertising work here, and what you can and can’t rely on in our materials.

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

You don’t need an account. You can use every lesson, game and test on this site without signing up or giving us your name.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you, and we don’t sell or share it.

Scores and answers in our tests and games are worked out in your browser. We don’t store them on a server.

Automatically collected information. Like most websites, we and our partners collect technical information when you visit, such as your browser and device type, approximate location (city or country), the pages you view and how you arrived at the site. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies, described below.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. This site uses them for two purposes:

Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful and how people find the site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as pages visited and time on site, reported to us in aggregate. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising. See the next section.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work, but ads may be less relevant to you.

Advertising

This site is free, and advertising helps keep it that way. We use Google AdSense to show ads.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Other approved third-party vendors and ad networks may also serve ads through Google on this site. Their use of cookies is covered by their own privacy policies, and many of them can be opted out of at www.aboutads.info.
  • If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they won’t be based on your browsing history.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland will be asked for consent before personalised ads or advertising cookies are used, through Google’s consent message. You can change your choice at any time using the privacy settings link shown with that message.

Advertising disclosure. Ads are chosen and placed automatically by Google. We don’t choose individual ads, and showing an ad doesn’t mean we endorse the product or advertiser. Anything marked Sponsored on this site is a paid or promotional placement.

Other services we use

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and some include embedded content such as videos or links to other sites. These services may receive your IP address and set their own cookies under their own privacy policies. We aren’t responsible for the content or privacy practices of external websites we link to.

Children

Our materials are used by English learners of many ages, often in classrooms. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. Teachers using the site with young learners can project it in class, so students don’t need to visit it on their own devices. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we’ll delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct any personal information we hold about you (in practice, only emails you’ve sent us), or ask us to delete it. Email modernenglishau@outlook.com. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
