Teacher Notes / Full Expression List (A1–A2)

▸ HAVE expressions

  • have breakfast – eat your morning meal
  • have lunch – eat your midday meal
  • have dinner – eat your evening meal
  • have a drink – drink something
  • have a shower – wash your body under running water
  • have a bath – wash your body in a bathtub
  • have a rest – stop and relax for a while
  • have a nap – sleep for a short time during the day
  • have a dream – experience images and stories while sleeping
  • have a headache – feel pain in your head
  • have a cold – be ill with a runny nose and sore throat
  • have a party – celebrate with friends and music
  • have a meeting – come together to talk about something
  • have a conversation – talk with another person
  • have fun – enjoy yourself
  • have a look – use your eyes to see something
  • have a go – try something
  • have an argument – disagree and speak angrily with someone
  • have a baby – give birth to a child
  • have time – be free to do something

▸ MAKE expressions

  • make breakfast – prepare the morning meal
  • make lunch – prepare the midday meal
  • make dinner – prepare the evening meal
  • make a sandwich – prepare a sandwich to eat
  • make a cup of tea – prepare tea in a cup
  • make a phone call – call someone on the telephone
  • make a mistake – do something wrong by accident
  • make a decision – choose what to do
  • make a plan – think about what you will do
  • make a list – write a number of things you need to do or buy
  • make noise – produce a loud sound
  • make friends – meet someone and become their friend
  • make money – earn money from work
  • make progress – improve or move forward
  • make an effort – try hard to do something
  • make a bed – tidy the sheets and pillows after sleeping
  • make a promise – say you will definitely do something
  • make a joke – say something funny
  • make an appointment – arrange a time to see someone
  • make a wish – hope for something to happen

▸ DO expressions

  • do homework – complete school work at home
  • do exercise – be physically active to stay healthy
  • do the dishes – wash the plates and cups after eating
  • do the laundry – wash your clothes
  • do the shopping – go to buy food and other things
  • do the cleaning – clean the house or room
  • do your best – try as hard as you can
  • do a course – study a subject for a period of time
  • do a job – complete a piece of work
  • do sport – play or practise a physical activity
  • do research – look for information about a topic
  • do a favour – help someone with something they need
  • do yoga – practise the exercise and stretching activity
  • do business – work in trade or commerce
  • do damage – cause harm to something
  • do well – perform in a good way
  • do badly – perform in a poor way
  • do nothing – not be busy; relax without activity
  • do someone a favour – help another person
  • do the ironing – press clothes to remove wrinkles

Write from Dictation

Press ▶ Listen to hear the sentence. Type the missing expression using have, make, or do, then press Submit.

Voice:

1) I always ______ before I go to work.

Answer: have breakfast

2) Everyone ______ sometimes. Don't worry!

Answer: make a mistake (or: makes a mistake)

3) I ______ after dinner every evening.

Answer: do homework

4) I ______ every morning before work.

Answer: have a shower

5) It's hard to ______ when you have many options.

Answer: make a decision

6) Can you please ______ after dinner tonight?

Answer: do the dishes

7) I always ______ when I go to the beach.

Answer: have fun

8) It's easy to ______ when you join a club.

Answer: make friends

9) Don't worry about the test — just ______.

Answer: do your best
