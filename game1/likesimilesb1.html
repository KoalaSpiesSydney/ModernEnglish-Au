Teacher Notes / Full Simile List (A2–B1)

These similes use verb + like + a/an + animal: She runs like a leopard = she runs very fast. The verb changes (runs, ran, is running) but the like part stays the same. Compare the A1–A2 similes with as … as: as brave as a lion.

  • run like a leopard – run very fast
  • swim like a fish – swim very well
  • fly like an eagle – fly high and strongly
  • sing like a bird – sing beautifully
  • glide like a swan – move smoothly and elegantly
  • eat like a horse – eat a lot
  • leap like a frog – jump suddenly forward
  • jump like a kangaroo – jump high, again and again
  • pounce like a tiger – jump suddenly to catch something
  • remember like an elephant – never forget anything
  • climb like a monkey – climb quickly and easily
  • work like a dog – work very hard
  • sting like a bee – hurt quickly and sharply, e.g. with words
  • float like a butterfly – move very lightly
  • waddle like a duck – walk with short steps, moving from side to side
  • slither like a snake – move smoothly along the ground, twisting
  • roar like a lion – shout very loudly
  • squeal like a pig – make a long, high, excited noise
  • crawl like a snail – move extremely slowly
  • howl like a wolf – cry out long and loud
  • charge like a bull – rush forward with great force

Notes: eat like a horse means eat a lot and is not rude. People also say eat like a pig (eat noisily and messily), but that is impolite about another person, though fine about yourself: I ate like a pig at the party! People often say have a memory like an elephant as well as remember like an elephant. Some verbs are close in meaning (leap, jump, pounce), so the game is a good chance to talk about the differences.

Complete the Sentences

Complete each sentence with an animal simile. Use the verb in brackets in the correct form (for example swims like a fish), then press Submit.

1) My sister trains for the swimming team every day. Now she ______ (swim).

Answer: swims like a fish

2) The winner of yesterday's race ______ (run).

Answer: ran like a leopard

3) Don't tell my grandmother anything you want her to forget. She ______ (remember).

Answer: remembers like an elephant

4) My little brother ______ (jump) on the trampoline all afternoon.

Answer: jumped like a kangaroo

5) The ice skater ______ (glide) across the ice.

Answer: glided like a swan

6) My dad works twelve hours a day, six days a week. He ______ (work).

Answer: works like a dog

7) When the cat saw the toy mouse, it ______ (pounce).

Answer: pounced like a tiger

8) Our choir's lead singer has a beautiful voice. She ______ (sing).

Answer: sings like a bird
