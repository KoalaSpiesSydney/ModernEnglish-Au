Teacher Notes / Full Simile List (A1–A2)

A simile compares two things using as … as: He is as brave as a lion = he is very brave. Remember an before a vowel sound: an ox, an owl.

  • as brave as a lion – not afraid of danger
  • as quiet as a mouse – making almost no noise
  • as sly as a fox – clever at tricking people
  • as busy as a bee – doing lots of things
  • as stubborn as a mule – not willing to change your mind
  • as blind as a bat – not able to see well
  • as gentle as a lamb – kind and soft
  • as proud as a peacock – very pleased with yourself
  • as hungry as a wolf – wanting a lot of food
  • as slow as a snail – moving very slowly
  • as playful as a kitten – liking to play and have fun
  • as strong as an ox – having great strength
  • as graceful as a deer – moving in a beautiful way
  • as fierce as a tiger – angry and frightening
  • as curious as a cat – wanting to know everything
  • as clumsy as a bear – often dropping or bumping into things
  • as cute as a bunny – small and sweet
  • as free as a bird – able to do what you want
  • as wise as an owl – knowing a lot; clever
  • as fat as a pig – very fat (rude about people!)

Note: as fat as a pig is a real simile, but it is rude to say about a person. Use it for animals or for a joke about yourself, e.g. After Christmas lunch I feel as fat as a pig!

Complete the Sentences

Read each sentence and type the missing animal simile (for example as busy as a bee), then press Submit.

1) The baby is sleeping, so please be ______.

Answer: as quiet as a mouse

2) My mum works all day and cooks every night. She is ______.

Answer: as busy as a bee

3) I can't read without my glasses. I'm ______!

Answer: as blind as a bat

4) My grandpa walks very slowly. He is ______.

Answer: as slow as a snail

5) The firefighter ran into the burning house. She was ______.

Answer: as brave as a lion

6) I didn't have lunch today, so now I'm ______.

Answer: as hungry as a wolf

7) The weightlifter can lift 200 kilograms. He is ______.

Answer: as strong as an ox

8) The ballet dancer moves so beautifully. She is ______.

Answer: as graceful as a deer
