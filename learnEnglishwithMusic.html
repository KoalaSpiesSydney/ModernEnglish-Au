Learn English with Music

Learn English with Music Original songs & classic songs turned into listening activities and grammar lessons. Play the track, fill in the words you hear, and check your answers as you go.

Tips for Using these ESL songs

1. Classroom karaoke — project the song onto the board with the teacher (or one of the students) as the host/presenter. Learn the lyrics and sing.

2. Group presentation — ideally on a tablet, but a phone or computer works too. One device goes on the table between a pair or group of students. They learn the song and present it to the class.

3. Homework — assign the song as homework, then open the next lesson with it.

Dance, Think, Flirt, Remember — All the Tenses
Theme: Latin pop · Level: B2–C1 · Listening gap-fill
How to use: one song, three languages, the same four verbs in every tense. Listen and type the English verbs into the gaps. The focus is when to use each tense, not only how to form it — the Spanish and Portuguese lines are there so you can see what your own language does at the same moment.
English · Español · Português

Sydney from a 747 by Paul Kelly performed by Merril Bainbridge — The Present Perfect for Experience
Theme: Australian pop · Level: A2–B1 · Pre-intermediate · Interactive video
How to use: watch the video and it stops eight times to ask about the story and the vocabulary (a 747, Bondi, the break, savings). Then practise Have you ever…? with the present perfect, past participles, and when to choose the past simple instead — and finish by asking a partner about their own experiences.
