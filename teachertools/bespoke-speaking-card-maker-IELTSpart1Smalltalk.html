How it works

  1. Choose topics, or type your own. Each topic becomes one card.
  2. Check the questions. On each card, ↻ New gives a different set, + Question adds one more (up to six) and ✎ Edit lets you change any question or write your own.
  3. Click Present on screen to show the cards on the board, one question at a time, and model the activity with the class.
  4. Print the same cards, cut along the dashed lines and hand them out for group work.

Good to know

  • Every card follows the pattern of IELTS Speaking Part 1: a question about now, one about the past, one asking for an opinion and one about the future.
  • The same pattern makes good small-talk practice. Students can ask each other the questions in pairs, then add their own follow-up questions.
  • To save a PDF instead of printing, choose "Save as PDF" in the print window.
  • For full-size cards, set the scale in the print window to 100% or "Actual size".

Questions

Where do the new questions come from?
They are built in your browser from the same four-step pattern as the teacher-written sets. Nothing is sent to an AI service or anywhere else.
Can I make a card for any topic?
Yes. Type it under "Your own topic" and choose what kind of topic it is, so the questions fit. Check the card before you print it.
Why can't I get new questions for some topics?
A few topics, such as pets and family, keep their teacher-written questions only, because they don't fit the pattern neatly.
