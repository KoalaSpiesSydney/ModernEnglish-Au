How it works

  1. Type a topic, then your pairs, one per line, with = in the middle.
  2. Choose 12, 16 or 24 tiles. Every round picks new pairs from your list.
  3. Press Play full screen to play on the board, Share with students for a link or QR code, or Download game to keep it as a file.

Ideas for class

  • Words and meanings: reluctant = not wanting to do something
  • Idioms: spill the beans = tell a secret
  • Collocations: fasten … = your seatbelt
  • Verb forms: go = went
  • Pictures: 🐘 = elephant
  • Questions and answers: How old are you? = I’m twelve.
  • On the board: turn on card numbers and let teams call out two numbers each turn.

Questions

How many pairs do I need?
At least one round’s worth: 6 pairs for 12 tiles, 8 for 16 tiles, 12 for 24 tiles. Add more and each round uses a different mix, so students can play again and again.
Do students need to sign up?
No. They scan the QR code or open the link and play straight away, on a phone, tablet or computer.
Is my game stored online?
No. The whole game travels inside the link, so nothing is uploaded or kept on a server. Very long lists may be too big for a QR code; send the link instead or download the game.
Can I keep the game?
Yes. Download game saves it as a single page you can open in any browser, put on your class website or send by email. Your list is also remembered in this browser for next time.
What if two cards could match the same card?
Students will be marked wrong for a sensible answer, so make each pair clearly different. The tool warns you when the same card appears twice.
