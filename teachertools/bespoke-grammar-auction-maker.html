How it works

  1. Give your auction a title and choose the type: spot the mistake, or true or false claims about a sentence.
  2. Type your sentences. Mark each one as correct or wrong, and add the correction. Explanations are optional.
  3. Choose a theme, then press Start the auction. The game fills the screen, ready for the projector.
  4. Use Download game to keep a copy that runs on any computer, even offline, or share it with colleagues.

Running the auction

  • The auctioneer (you or a student) reads each lot aloud and taps a team's paddle when they bid.
  • Teams bid on sentences they think are correct. Forgeries hide a mistake.
  • The reveal comes after the last lot. The class votes, then the stamp shows Genuine or Forgery.
  • Redemption: a team that bought a forgery scores double if it fixes the mistake.
  • Genuine = 1 point · corrected forgery = 2 points. Most points tops the Rich List.

Questions

How many sentences should I write?
Eight to twelve works well for a lesson. Aim for about half with mistakes, so teams can't win by bidding on everything.
What does "Highlight at the reveal" do?
The words you list are shaded at the reveal, so students can see what the sentence turned on. The wrong word in a forgery is crossed out automatically.
Are my sentences uploaded anywhere?
No. The auction runs in your browser and nothing you type is sent to us.
Will it remember my auction?
Yes, on this device and browser. To move it to another computer, use Save file, then Open file there, or download the game.
Can students play on their own devices?
The auction is designed for one screen at the front of the room, with teams bidding out loud.
