How it works

  1. Choose cue cards from the list, or write your own. Each card has a topic, three points to cover and a final "explain" point.
  2. On each card, ↻ Swap changes it for another card from the same theme and ✎ Edit lets you change any line.
  3. Click Present on screen to show a card on the board. Start the timer: students prepare, then the timer moves straight on to the speaking time.
  4. Print the same cards, cut along the dashed lines and hand them out for pair or group practice.

Good to know

  • The default timing follows IELTS Speaking Part 2: one minute to prepare and make notes, then up to two minutes of speaking.
  • Shorter or longer times suit other monologue practice, such as short talks or fluency rounds. Change them under Timer.
  • In pairs, one student speaks while the other keeps time and notes which points were covered. Then they swap.
  • To save a PDF instead of printing, choose "Save as PDF" in the print window. For full-size cards, set the scale to 100% or "Actual size".

Questions

Where do the cards come from?
They are teacher-written and follow the Part 2 pattern. They are not past exam papers.
Can I use the timer without the cards on screen?
Yes. Present any card and use the timer with your own topic; the timer settings stay the same for every card.
Will my cards be saved?
Your timer and layout settings are saved on this device. The cards are not, so print or save a PDF of a set you want to keep.
