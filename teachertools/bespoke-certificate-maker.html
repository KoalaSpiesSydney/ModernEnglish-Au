Questions

Is my class list uploaded anywhere?
No. The certificate is drawn in your browser. Names and logos stay on your device.
Is this an official certificate?
No. It is a certificate you design and give yourself. It is not an accredited qualification, and it must only be used for genuine awards. See the terms of use.
Can I add a signature?
Sign by hand, either with a pen after printing or with the Sign tool in your PDF reader. The maker leaves a line for each signer and does not store signatures.
What is the certificate number for?
Each certificate gets its own number. Download the record of names and numbers and keep it, so you can confirm later that a certificate came from you.
Will it remember my design?
Yes, on this device and browser. Your next visit opens with the same logo, colours and wording. Students' names are never kept: they are gone when you close the page.
What size does it print?
A4 or US Letter, wide or tall, at print quality.
