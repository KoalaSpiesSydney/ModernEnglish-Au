How it works

  1. Pick a ready-made list or type your own words, one per line.
  2. Choose the grid size and how many cards you need. Every card is shuffled differently.
  3. Download the PDF and print. The last page is a caller's list with your words in a random order.

Ideas for class

  • Definitions: read out a meaning; students mark the word.
  • Verb forms: put past forms on the cards and call the base form ("go" for "went").
  • Opposites: call "hot"; students find "cold".
  • Listening: read a short story and students mark each word they hear.
  • Numbers: use the 1 to 75 list for quick number practice.

Questions

How many words do I need?
At least enough to fill one card: 9 for a 3 × 3 card, 16 for 4 × 4 and 25 for 5 × 5, or one fewer on 3 × 3 and 5 × 5 cards with a free space. Add more and the cards differ more, so the game lasts longer.
Is every card different?
Yes. Each card gets its own random mix of your words in a random order.
Are my words uploaded anywhere?
No. The cards are drawn in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us.
Can I use phrases or short sentences?
Yes. Longer text is shrunk and wrapped to fit its square. Short entries print larger and are easier to read.
Will it remember my list?
Yes, on this device and browser, so your next visit opens with the same words and design.
What do students mark the cards with?
A pencil works for one game. For reuse, laminate the cards or use counters, buttons or scraps of paper.
