Custom tools for your school

The free tools in the Teacher Tool Directory, rebuilt around your own courses, levels and branding, so your teachers spend less time making materials and more time teaching.

What I can build for you

  • A level test for your coursesA placement or level check that matches your own levels and course names, with your logo, a QR code for students, and a paper version. Like the grammar level check, but yours.
  • Speaking and writing practiceSpeaking cards and essay questions built from your syllabus or exam focus, for IELTS, PTE, Cambridge or your own course. See the speaking card maker and the essay writer.
  • Interactive lessonsVideo, song and micro drama lessons that pause for questions, made from your own materials or chosen clips. See the video lesson maker.
  • Worksheets, certificates and reportsMakers for gap-fills, bingo and sentence scrambles, plus certificates and reports in your school's design and wording.
  • Something newA tool your teachers keep wishing existed. Tell me the problem and I'll suggest what we could build.

Why schools choose browser tools

  • No subscriptionsYou pay once for the build. There's no monthly licence per teacher.
  • No student data storedThe tools run in the browser. Nothing your teachers or students type is uploaded or kept online, which keeps privacy simple.
  • You keep the filesYou get the finished tool to host on your own website or learning platform, and you can keep using it for as long as you like.
  • Works on any devicePhones, tablets, laptops and classroom screens, with no apps to install and no logins.

How it works

  1. A short chatYou tell me what your teachers need, who your students are, and how the tool will be used.
  2. A clear quoteI send you a plan, a price and a timeline. No surprises.
  3. Build and tryI build a first version for your teachers to try, and adjust it based on their feedback.
  4. Hand overYou get the finished files, set up on your site or ready to upload, with a short guide for teachers.
