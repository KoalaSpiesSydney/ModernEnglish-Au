How it works

  1. Choose what to fold: a Christmas tree, a 5-point star, Santa or a bauble.
  2. Choose your colours and a pattern, and the size of the squares.
  3. Print the squares and the “How to fold it” page. Cut out the squares, then fold. No glue, no more cutting: just folding.

Ideas for class

  • Follow instructions: one student reads the tutorial or watches the video, and a partner folds. Then swap.
  • Teach a partner: students teach the folds with First, Then, Next, Finally.
  • Folding words: before you start, match the words on “How to fold it” (fold, crease, unfold, turn over) to actions.
  • Colour in: print outline patterns and let students colour their paper before they fold.
  • A class display: hang the stars and baubles on a tree, or stick the Santas and trees on a card.

Questions

Is this real origami?
Yes. Each model folds from one uncut square, with no glue. You only cut the squares out of the printed page.
Why is the back white?
Origami paper is usually coloured on one side and white on the other. Some models use both sides: Santa is red and white.
Which size should I choose?
15 cm is the usual size for these models. Bigger squares are easier for young learners and first tries.
My bauble won't blow up.
The paper may be too thick. Try thinner paper, and blow gently into the small hole.
