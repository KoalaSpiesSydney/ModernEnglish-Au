      How it works

      1. Paste the TikTok link of each episode, in order. They play on the right.
      2. Pause an episode where you want a question and pick a question type. Each question stops the episode at that moment.
      3. End an episode with a cliffhanger question. Students guess what happens next, then watch the next episode to find out.
      4. Students scan the QR code in the top-left corner. Click it, or the Share options button under it, to copy the link, show the QR code on the board or keep your teacher link. Click it again, or the ✕, to hide them. Students answer as they watch and see their score at the end.

      Ideas for class

      • Predict: micro dramas end every episode on a cliffhanger. Use it: "What happens next? Why do you think so?"
      • Who's who: early in episode 1, match each character to who they are (her boss, his ex, the secret sister…).
      • Feelings: pause on a face and ask how the character feels: furious, embarrassed, suspicious, relieved.
      • What did they say? Gap one key line of dialogue. Short and fast is fine: one line is enough.
      • Your line: a short-answer question: "What would you say back to him?" Then compare polite, rude and sarcastic answers.
      • After watching: students write and act out the next episode.
      • Aussie English: clips from Australian shows like Neighbours are great for accents and everyday expressions.

      Questions

      Which videos can I use?
      Episodes posted on TikTok by the show's official account, or by a creator who owns them. Look for the blue tick ✓ next to the account name. Reposts by fans are often removed, and then the lesson stops working.
      Do students need a TikTok account?
      No. They open the link or scan the QR code and start watching.
      The video won't load at school. Why?
      Many school networks block TikTok. Check the lesson on your school's network before class. Students can also use it at home.
      How do I save a lesson?
      Press Save to My lessons. The lesson is saved in this browser and the list of all your saved lessons opens. To keep a copy anywhere else, use My lessons › Download this lesson.
      Where is my lesson stored?
      On your own computer and inside the links you share. Nothing is uploaded to this site.
      Can I see my students' scores?
      Students see their own score at the end. They can copy their results and send them to you.
      Are micro dramas suitable for my class?
      Many are written for adults, with romance, revenge and some violence. Watch the whole series before you choose it.
