1 · Must, might or can’t?

In a drama we guess all the time. These modals of deduction show how sure we are.
must + verb: I’m sure it’s true.  She’s crying. Something must be wrong.
might / could / may + verb: maybe it’s true.  He might be her brother.
can’t + verb: I’m sure it’s NOT true.  That can’t be her husband. He’s in prison!

Choose the best word.

She drives a Ferrari and lives in a mansion. She be very rich.

I don’t know where she is. She be at work, or at her mum’s.

That be his wife. His wife is in London!

It’s 3 a.m. and all the lights are off. They be asleep.

He says he’s poor, but he wears a $20,000 watch. He be telling the truth.

The letter has no name on it. It be from her secret admirer.

2 · How do they feel?

Choose the feeling for each situation.

Your boyfriend says he’s “working late” again, and he smells of perfume.

You trip and fall in front of the whole office.

Someone breaks your phone and then laughs at you.

The doctor says the test results are fine.

The person you love marries someone else.

Your sister gets the job you wanted, and everyone praises her.

3 · Your turn · Talk or write

Finish these sentences about the drama. Then compare with a partner.

  • Mrs Mangel must be… because…
  • Henry might… because…
  • Madge can’t be happy, because…
  • In the next episode, Charlene might…

4 · Write the next scene

In pairs, write the next scene: one minute, two or three characters (Mrs Mangel must be one!), and it must end on a cliffhanger. Then act it out for the class. The class guesses what happens next!

Talk about it

  • Soap operas like Neighbours ran for years, with a cliffhanger every day. Today, micro dramas on TikTok do the same in one minute. Why do you think people love cliffhangers?
  • Would you pay to watch the next episode? Why, or why not?
  • Is there a nosy neighbour like Mrs Mangel where you live? What do they do?
