How it works

  1. Paste a YouTube link. The video plays on the right.
  2. Pause it where you want a question and pick a question type. Each question stops the video at that moment.
  3. Press Share with students and copy the link, or show the QR code on the board. Students answer as they watch and see their score at the end.

Ideas for class

  • Before watching: put a prediction question at 0:00 so students think about the topic first.
  • Listening for detail: make gap-fills from the transcript for key phrases and vocabulary.
  • Check understanding: add a multiple-choice question after each main idea.
  • Vocabulary: after the video, add a matching question with new words and their meanings.
  • Sequencing: use "Put in order" for the steps of a process or the events in a story.
  • Discussion: finish with a short-answer question at the end for students to share in pairs.
  • No devices? Print the worksheet, play the video for the whole class and pause it yourself.

Questions

Do students need an account?
No. They open the link or scan the QR code and start watching.
How do I save a lesson?
Press Save. The lesson goes into My lessons in this browser, ready to open next time. To keep a copy anywhere else, use My lessons › Download this lesson: you get a small file that opens the lesson.
Where is my lesson stored?
On your own computer and inside the links you share. Nothing is uploaded to this site.
How do I change a lesson later?
When you share, keep the teacher link. It opens the lesson ready to change on any computer. Then share the new student link.
What if a student closes the page?
Their answers are kept on their device. When they open the link again on the same device, they can carry on or start again.
Can I see my students' scores?
Students see their own score at the end. They can copy their results and send them to you, for example in your class chat or learning platform.
Why won't a video play?
Some owners don't allow their videos to play on other websites. Choose another video, or one from a channel that allows sharing.
Can I get the transcript automatically?
YouTube only lets a video's owner download its captions, so copy the transcript from YouTube and paste it in. It takes about 30 seconds.
