How it works

  1. Paste your text: a reading passage, a dialogue or a list of sentences.
  2. Click the words to blank out, or let the tool pick every 7th word, random words, or all the articles or prepositions.
  3. Download the PDF and print. The last page is the answer key.

Ideas for class

  • Grammar focus: blank every article or preposition to practise one point.
  • Vocabulary review: blank this week's new words and keep the word box.
  • Listening: read the text aloud; students fill the gaps as they hear them.
  • Mixed levels: print one copy with the word box and one with first letters only.
  • Reading test: blank every 7th word with no word box for a classic cloze test.

Questions

What is a gap-fill or cloze worksheet?
A text with some words taken out. Students read it and write the missing words, with or without a list to choose from.
Is my text uploaded anywhere?
No. The worksheet is drawn in your browser. Nothing you type or paste is sent to us.
How many gaps should I make?
About one word in seven to ten keeps a text readable. Leaving the first sentence complete helps students get into the topic.
How long can the text be?
Up to about 2,000 words. Longer texts run onto extra pages automatically.
Why are some words in the word box in small letters?
A word that starts a sentence is shown without its capital letter, so the capital does not give the answer away.
Will it remember my worksheet?
Yes, on this device and browser, so your next visit opens with the same text and gaps.
