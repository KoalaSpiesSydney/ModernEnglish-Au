How it works

  1. Pick a ready-made set or type your own sentences, one per line.
  2. Choose slashes or boxes, and how much help to give: the capital letter, the first word, the end punctuation.
  3. Download the PDF and print. The last page is the answer key.

Ideas for class

  • Grammar review: use sentences with this week's structure, such as questions or the present perfect.
  • Word groups: add slashes to keep phrases together for lower levels, or to practise collocations.
  • Cut and sort: choose boxes and a large text size, then cut the words out for pairs to arrange.
  • Race: teams unscramble on the board; the first correct sentence wins a point.
  • Error correction: scramble sentences from your students' own writing, corrected.

Questions

What is a sentence scramble?
A sentence with its words in a jumbled order. Students put the words back in order and write the sentence. It is also called a jumbled sentence or word-order exercise.
Are my sentences uploaded anywhere?
No. The worksheet is drawn in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us.
How many sentences can I use?
Up to 40. About eight to ten fit on a page, and longer sets run onto extra pages.
Why does the first word lose its capital letter?
So the capital does not show which word comes first. Tick "Keep the capital letter" if a sentence starts with a name, or if you want to give that clue.
Can I keep a phrase together?
Yes. Put a slash between the groups of words in that sentence and each group stays in one piece.
Will it remember my sentences?
Yes, on this device and browser, so your next visit opens with the same worksheet.
