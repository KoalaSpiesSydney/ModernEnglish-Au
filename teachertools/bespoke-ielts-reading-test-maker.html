Questions for Reading Passage 1

A real test has 40 questions, usually 13 or 14 for each passage, in two or three different types. The instructions and numbering follow the IELTS style automatically. Tip: select words in the passage, then press + From selected text.

Tip: let an AI assistant write the questions

  1. Choose how many questions you want and press Copy the questions prompt.
  2. Paste it into Claude or your AI assistant and send it.
  3. Copy the whole reply and press Paste the questions. They appear below, ready to check and edit.

Test settings, print and project

IELTS-style Academic Reading

Your teacher will go through the answers with you.

How it works

  1. Add Reading Passage 1 from a link, a file or pasted text, and check its level.
  2. Add questions, write them yourself or paste them from an AI assistant. Numbering and instructions are done for you.
  3. Add Passage 2 and Passage 3 with the tabs at the top. Numbering carries on across the whole test.
  4. Print the test with an answer sheet and answer key, show it on the board with a timer, or press Share with students to make a link or QR code so they can take it on their phones and check their answers.

On the board

  • The passage appears on the left and its questions on the right, like the computer-delivered test.
  • Switch passages with the Part tabs or the keys 1 to 3. Start and pause the timer with the button or the space bar.
  • Change the text size with A+ and A− (or + and −), and reveal the answers with Answers (or A). Press QR (or Q) to put up a code for students. Press Esc to go back.

Question types

  • True / False / Not Given and Yes / No / Not Given
  • Multiple choice
  • Matching headings, matching information, matching features and matching sentence endings
  • Sentence completion, summary completion (with or without a word box) and short answer
