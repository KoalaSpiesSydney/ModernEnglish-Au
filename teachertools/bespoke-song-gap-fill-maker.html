How it works

  1. Paste the lyrics of the song you want to use. Keep the line breaks and the empty lines between verses.
  2. Click the words to blank out, or let the tool pick the rhyming words at the end of the lines.
  3. Add the song title and a link, then download the PDF. Play the song in class while students fill the gaps.

Ideas for class

  • Predict first: students guess the missing words before they listen, then check with the song.
  • Rhymes: blank the rhyming words. Students use the sound of the line before to help them.
  • Grammar in songs: blank every preposition, or every -ing word.
  • Mixed levels: print one copy with the word box and one with first letters only.
  • After listening: add two or three questions about the meaning and the singer's feelings.

Questions

Does this site provide song lyrics?
No. You paste the lyrics yourself. Apart from the out-of-copyright example, this site has no lyrics of its own.
Are the lyrics I paste saved or uploaded?
No. They are used only in your browser window and are never sent to us. They are not saved on your device either: when you close or leave the page, they are gone.
What is remembered?
Only your design choices, such as the gap style, colour and font, so they are ready next time.
What about chorus lines that repeat?
Tick "Gap a repeated line only the first time" so the same word is not a gap five times.
Why are section names like [Chorus] shown differently?
Labels such as [Chorus], Verse 2: or (Bridge) are kept as headings and are never turned into gaps.
