    How it works

    1. Give the test a title, then add your questions. Mix the five question types as you like.
    2. Choose the scoring. "1 point each" is a normal test. "Level test" puts the questions in order of difficulty and stops after a few wrong answers in a row.
    3. Download the PDF or print it. For the online test, copy the student link and share it, or put its QR code on the paper.

    Question types

    • Multiple choice: a sentence with a gap and two to four answers.
    • Gap fill: students write the missing word. Add a word in brackets for verb forms.
    • Correct or incorrect: students decide if a sentence is right, and correct it if not.
    • Word order: the words are mixed up for you. Students put them back in order.
    • Rewrite the sentence: a key word transformation, like in Cambridge exams.

    Questions

    How does the online test mark written answers?
    It ignores capital letters, extra spaces and the full stop at the end. Anything else must match one of your answers, so list every answer you accept, separated by /.
    The QR code for the online test is very busy. Why?
    The whole test is inside the link, so long tests make long links. Shorten the student link with a link shortener, then choose "My own link" and paste the short link.
    Where is my test saved?
    Only in this browser on this device. To keep a copy, move it to another computer or share it with a colleague, use "Save test file".
