How it works

  1. Add your logo and change the title, details and sections to match your school's report.
  2. Paste your class list.
  3. Download. You get one Word file per student, with the name and details filled in, ready for you to type or handwrite comments.

Good to know

  • One student gives you a single Word file. Two or more arrive together in a ZIP folder.
  • The grey example text is a guide for the writer. Delete it in Word, or switch it off for a section by clearing its example box.
  • Your layout is remembered on this device, so next term you only change the class list.

Questions

Are my students' names uploaded anywhere?
No. The Word files are made in your browser. Names and logos stay on your device, and the class list is forgotten when you close the page unless you tick "Remember this class list".
Can I open the reports without Word?
Yes. They are standard .docx files, which also open in Google Docs, Pages and LibreOffice.
Can I write the comments here?
Not yet. This tool builds the report for each student; you write the comments in Word.
