Sponsor these tools

Reach English teachers while they're planning lessons. Sponsorship keeps the Teacher Tool Directory free for every teacher, with no sign-up and no subscriptions.

Who uses the tools

  • English teachersESL, ELICOS, adult education and school teachers who make their own lessons, worksheets and tests.
  • Exam preparationTeachers and students preparing for IELTS, PTE and Cambridge exams.
  • In class, on the big screenMany tools are used live in lessons, projected for the whole class or shared by QR code.

Ask me for current visitor numbers and where visitors come from.

What sponsors get

  • A sponsor boxA simple, clearly labelled box with your name, a short message and a link, on the Teacher Tool Directory and the tool pages.
  • One sponsor per areaSponsor a group of tools, such as exam preparation or interactive lessons, without competitors alongside you.

What I accept

Teachers trust these tools, so I'm careful about sponsors. I work with reputable colleges, universities, exam providers, publishers and education companies whose products I'm happy to recommend to teachers. Sponsor boxes are clearly labelled, never pop up, and never collect visitors' data.

Talk to me about sponsoring

Tell me about your organisation and the teachers you'd like to reach. I'll send you visitor numbers, the available spaces and prices.
