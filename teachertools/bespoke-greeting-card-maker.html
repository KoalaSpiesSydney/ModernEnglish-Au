      How it works

      1. Choose an occasion. The greeting and message are filled in for you.
      2. Choose a vintage picture or a drawing to colour in, leave the front blank for students to draw on, or add your own picture. Choose your frame, fonts and colours.
      3. Choose how to fold it, and your paper. Print, fold, and give it to someone.

      Ideas for class

      • Colour in: choose “Colour in” and print a class set. Students colour the frame and the letters.
      • Draw your own: the front is blank for students' own pictures.
      • Writing practice: choose “Sentence starters” inside. Students finish each sentence, then read their card to a partner.
      • A card for everyone: paste your class list into “Make one card for each name”.
      • Follow instructions: print “How to fold” and let students fold their own cards. Fold, turn over, press…

      Questions

      Which fold should I choose?
      “In quarters” prints on one side, so any printer can do it. “In half” makes a bigger card but needs double-sided printing (or you can leave the inside blank and write by hand).
      Why is half the page upside down?
      On a card folded in quarters, the top half is printed upside down so it's the right way up when you fold it.
      The inside is upside down, or on the wrong side.
      Try the other flip setting (long edge or short edge) in your printer's double-sided options. Print one card first to check.
      Can I send a card by email?
      Yes. Open “The finished card” and choose Download PNG for pictures of the front, inside and back.
