A note to teachers

Every tool on this page is free for teachers. There's no sign-up, no subscription and no account to create.

Your work belongs to you

Nothing you type is uploaded to us or stored in any cloud. The lessons, worksheets and tests you make are yours: download them, keep them on your own device, reuse them and change them whenever you like. Some tools remember your drafts in your own browser, on your own computer, so you don't lose your work; others forget everything the moment you close the page. Either way, we never see it, and it's never kept anywhere but with you.

Why I built them this way

Teachers often pay for several subscriptions to several tools that each do just one thing. Much of that money pays to store teachers' own work: their own intellectual property. And the clouds it is stored in cost water and electricity. I'd rather these tools set you free, so you can spend your time and energy on creative, innovative, up-to-date lessons that belong to you.

AI as a companion, not a replacement

I love AI as a companion tool. But AI cannot replace a teacher. It can't bring compassion, presence or passion to a classroom, and it can't respond to the world as it is right now, or to the moments that happen between a teacher and their students.

Many edtech companies are gathering what teachers know in the hope of replacing teachers with AI. Let's turn the tables.

Instead of collecting the tricks of teaching to replace the teacher, let teachers use AI to replace the developer.

That's how these tools were made. Let's use AI ethically, as our friend.
