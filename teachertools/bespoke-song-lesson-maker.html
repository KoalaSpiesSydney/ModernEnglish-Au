    How it works

    1. Paste the YouTube link for the song. It plays on the right.
    2. Paste the lyrics and click the words to blank out, or let the tool pick the rhymes.
    3. Pause the video where you want a question and pick a question type. Each question stops the song at that moment.
    4. Press Share with students and copy the link, or show the QR code. Students fill the gaps as they listen, answer the questions and see their score at the end.

    Ideas for class

    • Before listening: put a prediction question at 0:00 about the title or the story.
    • Rhymes: gap the rhyming words. Students use the sound of the line before to help them.
    • Mixed levels: share one link with a word box and one with first letters only.
    • Grammar in songs: gap every past tense verb, then add a retell gap-fill after the song.
    • Vocabulary: after the song, add a matching question with new words and their meanings.
    • Story songs: use "Put in order" for the events in the song.
    • No devices? Print the worksheet, play the song for the whole class and pause it yourself. Hand out the lyric sheet afterwards to sing along.

    Questions

    Does this site provide song lyrics?
    No. You paste the lyrics yourself. Apart from the out-of-copyright example, this site has no lyrics of its own.
    Do students need an account?
    No. They open the link or scan the QR code and start listening.
    How do I save a lesson?
    Press Save. The lesson goes into My lessons in this browser. To keep a copy anywhere else, use My lessons › Download this lesson.
    Where is my lesson stored?
    On your own computer and inside the links you share. Nothing is uploaded to this site.
    What if a student closes the page?
    Their answers are kept on their device. When they open the link again on the same device, they can carry on or start again.
    Can I see my students' scores?
    Students see their own score at the end. They can copy their results and send them to you.
    Why won't a video play?
    Some owners, often record labels, don't allow their videos to play on other websites. Try another upload of the song, such as an official lyric video.
