How it works

  1. Choose the card you made: which paper, and folded in half or in quarters. The envelope is sized to fit it.
  2. Choose a pocket or diamond envelope, a stamp, and the words on the front.
  3. Print at “Actual size”, cut along the solid line, and fold along the dashed lines.

Which paper?

A card from A4 folded in quarters (A6)
The envelope prints on A4.
A card from A4 folded in half (A5)
The envelope prints on A3.
Letter cards
Quarter-fold cards print on Letter; half-fold cards print on Tabloid (11 × 17 in).
Which paper weight?
Ordinary 80–120 gsm paper folds best. Thick card is hard to fold neatly.

Ideas for class

  • Following instructions: print “How to fold” and let students fold their own envelope, step by step.
  • Addresses: practise writing a name and address on the lines, then “post” the cards to classmates.
  • A class set: paste your class list into “Make one envelope for each name”.
