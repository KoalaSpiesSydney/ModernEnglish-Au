How it works

  1. Paste a text, upload a Word file, PDF or photo of a page, or paste a link to a web page.
  2. Choose your class level. Harder words are highlighted in yellow, harder grammar in purple, and long sentences are underlined.
  3. Edit the text right there, replacing hard words, simplifying grammar and splitting long sentences, until the level check is right. Or copy the ready-made prompt into Claude or another AI assistant to rewrite it for you, then paste the new version back.
  4. Add key words, tick the exercises you want and write your questions.
  5. Print the student sheet and answer key, or press Show on board.

On the board

  • Switch between Text, Vocabulary, Exercises and Questions with the tabs or the keys 1 to 4.
  • Make the words bigger or smaller with A+ and A− (or the + and − keys).
  • Reveal the answers when you are ready with Answers (or the A key). Press Esc to go back.

The level check

  • Every word is matched against a list of the 16,000 most common English words. The more common a word, the lower its level.
  • Grammar structures are found automatically, from the past continuous (A2) to inversion and cleft sentences (C1), and each one is given its usual level.
  • The final level combines vocabulary, grammar and sentence length. Names of people and places are left out.
  • It is a quick guide, not an exact measure, so trust your own judgement as well.

Good to know

  • Everything happens on your own device. Your text is never uploaded, even when you use a photo.
  • When you get a text from a web link, the link is sent to the free Jina Reader service, which fetches the page and sends back its text.
  • Your work is saved in this browser, so you can close the page and come back to it.
  • Scanned PDFs have no text inside them. Take a photo of the page instead.
