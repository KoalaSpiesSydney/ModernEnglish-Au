      How it works

      1. The 48 questions are in order of difficulty: eight at each level from A1 (beginner) to C2 (proficiency).
      2. The test stops after three wrong answers in a row. The level of the question where it stops is the student's grammar level.
      3. Each correct answer earns points by level: 1 point at A1, up to 6 points at C2. The best possible score is 168.

      Questions

      How do I use the paper test?
      Students answer every question they can, in order. Mark from question 1 and stop at the third wrong answer in a row. A blank counts as wrong. The answer key page explains the scoring.
      Where does the QR code go?
      To this page, so students can do the test on a phone or tablet instead of on paper. You can also show it on the classroom screen.
      Are the results saved?
      No. Names and answers stay in the student's browser window. To keep a result, use "Print my results" (or save it as a PDF from the print window), or "Copy my results" and paste it into an email.
