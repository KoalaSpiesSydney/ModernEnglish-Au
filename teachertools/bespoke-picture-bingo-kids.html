Bingo

How it works

  1. Choose one or more themes and a level. Skip the words you don't want, or add your own.
  2. Choose what to make: bingo boards, cards, a caller's list or a how-to-play sheet.
  3. Download a PDF to print, or PNG pictures for slides and your learning platform. Every bingo board is different.

Games to play

  • Bingo: call words from the caller's list, or show the pictures on the board with "Call bingo on screen".
  • Memory: print pairs (a picture card and a word card) and play in small groups.
  • Snap and Go Fish: print two sets of pairs. Students ask "Do you have a…?"
  • Flashcards: print "picture front, word back" on A3 to show the whole class.
  • Colour and draw: choose black and white, and students colour the pictures in.

Questions

Is it really free?
Yes. There's no sign-up, and nothing you choose or type is sent to us.
Where do the pictures come from?
They are Noto Emoji, made by Google and free to use under the Apache License 2.0.
Why are the playing cards the wrong size?
Check that your printer is set to "Actual size" or 100%, not "Fit to page".
The backs don't line up with the fronts.
Try the other flip setting (long edge or short edge) in step 2, and in your printer.
