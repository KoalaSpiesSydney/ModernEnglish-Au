How it works

  1. In Teacher mode, open the Question bank and click Use next to a question, or write your own.
  2. Press Start and write in the answer box. The timer counts down and the word count updates as you type.
  3. Work through the essay checklist. Turn on the spell or grammar check under the answer box when you want to review.
  4. Project the question with Show on board, share it with the QR code, or print it.

Using it with a class

  • In class: choose Teacher at the top, click Show on board to project the question, then press Sample essay to show a model answer and talk it through.
  • Homework: leave the QR code on the board (press Q). Students scan it, and the same question opens in Student mode on their phone or computer.
  • Handing in: students write their name, write the essay and press Download my essay. They send you the PDF, which shows their name, the date, the word count and the time taken.
  • On paper: print the question with writing lines and hand it out.

Question categories

  • Argument: agree or disagree with a statement, or decide if a change is positive or negative.
  • Discussion: discuss two views and give your own opinion.
  • Comparing: weigh up two options, or advantages and disadvantages.
  • Suggesting: explain a problem and suggest solutions.
  • Explaining: answer two questions, such as causes and effects.

Questions

Can I add my own questions?
Yes. In Teacher mode, click Question bank under the question menus. Use "Export bank" to keep a copy or move your questions to another computer.
How do I show a sample essay?
In Teacher mode, paste it into the Sample essay box, then click Show on board and press Sample essay.
How does the grammar check work?
It looks for common learner mistakes, such as articles, subject–verb agreement and uncountable nouns. It is a guide, not a full grammar checker.
Where is my writing saved?
Only in this browser on this device. Use Download my essay to keep a copy.
