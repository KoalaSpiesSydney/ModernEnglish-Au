Do, does, is or are?

Asking for personal information. Learn the rule, practise it, then ask a partner. Project it for the class, play it on a phone, or print the worksheet.

Remember

Look at the word after the gap. Is there another verb?

is / are

No other verb. Use be with a noun, an adjective, an age or a place.

  • Are you a student?
  • Is your sister married?
  • Where are you from?

do / does

There is another verb: live, work, have, like, speak …

  • Do you live near here?
  • Does your sister work?
  • Where do you work?

is and does go with he, she, it or one person or thing. are and do go with you, we, they or more than one.

      C. Ask a partner

      Say the question with the missing word. Then ask your partner. You can invent your answers.

      Listen to the answer, then ask one more question.

      D. Tell the class

      Say three or four things about your partner. Remember the s: he lives, she speaks, he has.

      • My partner is from … and lives in …
      • She has two brothers. / He doesn't have any brothers or sisters.
      • His first language is … and he speaks …
      • Her hobbies are … and she likes …
