C1 Advanced · Timed · Instant Feedback

Mini Cambridge C1 Advanced tests covering Reading and Use of English, plus Listening and Writing. Each one is a 30-minute snapshot of the real exam — use it as a quick warm-up, a mid-course check, or a short diagnostic before students commit to a full CAE course. The skills, themes and vocabulary also carry straight over to IELTS and PTE.

As a lesson

Project it on the board and work through it with the class, from the pre-lessons to the question skills. Students can scan the QR code to open their own copy on their device.

As a warm-up

Project it, divide the class into teams and keep score on the board, giving feedback after each question.

As a weekly test

Students see their score on their phone — ask them to show you, or send you a screenshot of their results.

As homework

Set any test for students to complete at home.

For self-study

Work through a test at your own pace without pressing Start, paying attention to the pre-lessons. When you're ready, press Start and do it again under timed conditions before the real exam.
