Respond to situation — apologising

Every situation on this page asks you to apologise and put something right. Respond politely and clearly. Include:

1) A clear apology at the start — say sorry and name what you are apologising for.
2) A brief explanation of what happened, without making excuses.
3) Responsibility and a fix — what you will do to put it right, and by when.
4) A polite closing — reassurance it will not happen again, or thanks for their understanding.

Useful language for apologising

Sincere and specific — the tone examiners expect.

Saying sorry

  • I'm really sorry about what happened yesterday.
  • I want to apologise for missing the deadline.
  • I owe you an apology, and I won't make excuses.
  • I'm very sorry for the inconvenience this has caused.

Explaining briefly

  • What happened was that I misread the schedule.
  • I'd written the wrong date in my calendar.
  • There was a mix-up with the files on my end.
  • That's an explanation, not an excuse.

Taking responsibility

  • It was my mistake, and I take full responsibility.
  • I should have checked before sending it.
  • To put it right, I'll … by Friday.
  • I've already started fixing it.

Reassuring and closing

  • I'll set a reminder so it doesn't happen again.
  • Please let me know if there's anything else I can do.
  • Thanks for being so understanding about it.
  • I really appreciate your patience.
