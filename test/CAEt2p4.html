Part 4 — Key word transformations

For questions 21–24, complete the second sentence so that it means the same as the first, using the word given. Do not change the word given. You must use between three and six words, including that word.

This is the only part worth two marks a question — one for each half of the answer — so a half-right answer still scores. Count your words before you move on.

Example (0) on

James would only speak to the head of department alone.

James insisted to the head of department alone.

21 may

The CEO said, “It is possible that the meeting will be postponed until after Christmas.”

The meeting until after Christmas.

22 recommended

“You should try the new Italian restaurant in town,” she said.

She Italian restaurant in town.

23 advised

“If you want to improve, you should practise every day,” the coach told me.

The coach every day to improve.

24 complained

“The order arrived late,” the client said.

The client arriving late.
