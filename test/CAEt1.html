Cambridge C1 Advanced · Reading & Use of English

Use of English — Test 1

  • Level C1
  • Theme: ancient civilisations
  • 3 parts · 24 questions
  • About 30 minutes

One theme runs through all three parts, so the same historical and philosophical vocabulary comes back at you in a different shape each time — first to recognise, then to produce. Do the parts in order the first time; after that, use them separately for whatever you find hardest.

Before you start

Read the hints sheet first. It pre-teaches the collocations and the preposition patterns this test relies on, and explains what each part is really testing — worth ten minutes before you open Part 1.

⬇ Hints & pre-teaching (PDF)

The test

Part1

Multiple-choice cloze

Eight gaps, four options each. Vocabulary: which word actually goes with the words around it — collocation, phrasal verbs, and shades of meaning between near-synonyms.

8 questions · about 10 minutes · recognition
Part2

Open cloze

Eight gaps, no options, one word in each. Grammar and structure: prepositions, articles, auxiliaries, relative pronouns and linking words — the small words that hold a sentence together.

8 questions · about 10 minutes · production
Part3

Word formation

Eight gaps, each with a stem word to change. Prefixes, suffixes and word class — and whether the sentence needs a negative, a plural, or a different part of speech entirely.

8 questions · about 10 minutes · production

In the real exam, Reading & Use of English is 90 minutes for eight parts, so Parts 1–3 are worth roughly 30 of those minutes. Time yourself only once you are getting the answers right — accuracy first, speed second.
