Cambridge C1 Advanced · Reading & Use of English

Use of English — Test 2

  • Level C1
  • Theme: ancient civilisations
  • 4 parts · 24 questions
  • About 35 minutes

The full Use of English section of the paper: vocabulary, grammar, word building and rephrasing, in the order Cambridge sets them. Do the parts in order the first time; after that, use them separately for whatever you find hardest.

The test

Part1

Multiple-choice cloze

Eight gaps, four options each. Vocabulary: which word actually goes with the words around it — collocation, phrasal verbs, and shades of meaning between near-synonyms.

8 questions · about 10 minutes · recognition
Part2

Open cloze

Eight gaps, no options, one word in each. Grammar and structure: prepositions, articles, auxiliaries, relative pronouns and linking words — the small words that hold a sentence together.

8 questions · about 10 minutes · production
Part3

Word formation

Each gap has its own stem word to change. Prefixes, suffixes and word class — and whether the sentence needs a negative, a plural, or a different part of speech entirely.

4 questions · about 5 minutes · production
Part4

Key word transformations

Sentences to rewrite using a given word, in three to six words, without changing the meaning. The part that catches people out: it tests grammar and vocabulary at the same time, and it is the only part worth two marks a question.

4 questions · about 8 minutes · production

A full Cambridge paper has 8, 8, 8 and 6 questions in these four parts. This practice test is shorter, so treat the timings as a guide rather than exam conditions. Time yourself only once you are getting the answers right — accuracy first, speed second.
