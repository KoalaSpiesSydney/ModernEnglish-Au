New PTE Speaking Question 2026 — Respond to Situation

The PTE 'Respond to a Situation' task builds your ability to react clearly and politely when someone gives you a problem or request. You show you understand the context, choose the right tone, and organise your message logically. This skill directly supports the IELTS General Training letter, where you also respond to a situation by explaining, requesting, apologising, or suggesting.
