Follow the blog by RSS

Get every new post from the Modern English Australia blog in a feed reader, an app that collects updates from all the sites you follow in one place. Free, and no email address needed.

The blog's feed address

https://modernenglish.au/feed.xml

Or follow in one click

Follow on Feedly Follow on Inoreader

How it works

  1. Get a feed reader. Feedly and Inoreader are free on the web and on phones. NetNewsWire is free on Mac and iPhone.
  2. Add the address. Paste https://modernenglish.au/feed.xml into the reader's “Add” or “Follow” box, or use a one-click button above.
  3. Read new posts there. Each new post appears in your reader automatically. To stop, just remove the feed. There's nothing to unsubscribe from.

Prefer video? There are how-to videos for the games on YouTube and TikTok.
